A live mission

Three drones, five people, ten seconds

43 s: five people reported within 4 s. CBBA gives each one to a drone; the bids are in the log.

52 s: all five reached, 6 s after the last report. Closest the drones came: 2.60 m.

This is one real flight, 3 PX4 drones in Gazebo on 21 October, drawn from above. Blue shading is ground searched recently; each line is a drone’s path; diamonds are people, coloured by the drone that won them. On the left, at 43 seconds, five people have been reported within four seconds and CBBA has already given each one to a drone; the log on the right shows the actual bids, for example person 2 went to drone 2 with a bid of 0.81. On the right, at 52 seconds, every person has a tick: all reached six seconds after the last report, and the drones have gone back to searching. Note drone 2 took three people in a row; that is CBBA favouring the total over the last person, which comes back under limitations.
