Search · when plans change

PSO adapts; the lawnmower can’t

Faster in 11 of 12 cases: 13–34% when a drone leaves, up to 42% if people also cluster by a river. The lawnmower is told instantly who left and re-plans.

Two scenarios test adaptation. In the first, the last drone hits its battery reserve 15 seconds in and leaves. In the second, a river crosses the area and most people are near it; the drones get the flood-risk map, the lawnmower does not use one. I gave the lawnmower its best chance: it re-divides the area the instant a drone leaves. PSO is still faster in 11 of 12 cases. These runs also exposed a weakness: with few drones, ground was marked due for a second search before everything had been searched once. A longer revisit time, with the oldest 10 percent of the area always due, fixed it: unfinished missions went from 27 out of 60 to none in the worst case.
