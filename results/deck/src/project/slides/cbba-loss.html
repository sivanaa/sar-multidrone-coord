CBBA · lost messages

Four fixes keep missions finishing when messages are lost

Re-broadcast every second, so a lost message is repeated.

Merge two reports of one person (within 1 m).

Refresh bids as a drone closes in, with a 20% margin.

Ignore stale claims relayed after their drone dropped them.

Plain CBBA, as published, broke in my live and simulated runs. A lost message was never repeated, two drones seeing the same person created two tasks, a drone’s bid froze when it won so a passing drone could steal the task mid-flight, and old claims kept being relayed. Each fix was measured on and off. Together, the missions where every person was reached stay at 80 out of 80 at every loss level, one mission short at 20 percent. Plain CBBA drops to 52 and 45 out of 80 at 60 percent loss.
