PSO keeps pace with a lawnmower

Both are 2–3× faster than random waypoints. Median with 3 drones: PSO 42.0 s, lawnmower 40.5 s; the lawnmower’s slowest missions finish sooner.

Same CBBA, safety and speed limits for every method; only the search differs. Each line shows the share of 60 missions where everyone was reached by a given time, in an area 12 metres in radius. On an empty map PSO and a lawnmower sweep are about level, and both are two to three times faster than random waypoints. The lawnmower has the better worst case because it guarantees covering everything once. I also tested the coverage map without the swarm motion and it does about as well, so the coverage map does most of the work.
