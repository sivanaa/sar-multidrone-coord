How it was tested

Measured in simulation, confirmed in flight

Fleet simulator

The real CBBA, PSO and safety code. Hundreds of missions in minutes, with message loss and delay.

Gazebo + PX4

Live flights with 2, 3 and 4 drones, after every change to the flight code.

AirSim + PX4

Unreal flood scene with a downward camera; 3-drone flights.

Baselines for every claim: the best possible assignment by brute force, and lawnmower and random search.

The method matters as much as the algorithms. Because the logic is separate from ROS, a fast simulator runs the exact flight code for hundreds of missions, with a vehicle model fitted to Gazebo and AirSim and a radio model with message loss. Every result was measured there and then confirmed with live PX4 flights. And every claim has a baseline: CBBA is compared with the brute-force optimum, and PSO with a lawnmower sweep and random search.
