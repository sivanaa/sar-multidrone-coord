How it works

The same node runs on every drone

Search: PSO

A coverage map of what has been seen, weighted by flood risk. Particle Swarm Optimisation moves each drone toward unsearched ground.

Assign: CBBA

Each person found becomes a task. Drones bid and reach agreement by messages alone (Choi et al., 2009).

Safety floor

Every setpoint keeps 1.8 m from where each neighbour is heading, with a braking limit.

ROS 2 Humble · PX4 SITL v1.18 · Gazebo Harmonic · AirSim 1.8.1. All decision logic sits in one ROS-free class, so the exact flight code also runs in a fast simulator.

Search uses a coverage map: which ground each drone has seen recently, optionally weighted by a flood-risk map. PSO moves the drones toward high-value unsearched ground. When anyone detects a person, it becomes a task, and CBBA, the Consensus-Based Bundle Algorithm, lets the drones bid and agree on who goes where using only messages. Under both sits a hard safety floor: every position command keeps 1.8 m from where each neighbour is predicted to be, and limits approach speed. A drone at low battery hands its tasks to the others and flies home.
