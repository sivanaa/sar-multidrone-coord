Coordinating a drone fleet for search and rescue

How 2 to 4 drones split up a search area and the people they find, with no central controller

Presenter

Sivana Terron Gonzalez

Supervisor

Dr. Pao-Ann Hsiung

Introduce yourself and the project. One sentence: a ROS 2 coordination layer that lets a small fleet of drones search a flooded area and decide among themselves who goes to which person they find. The picture is a real 3-drone flight in Gazebo, seen from above: each coloured line is a drone, and each tick is a person that drone reached. Everything shown was measured in a fast fleet simulator and then flown with PX4 drones in Gazebo and AirSim.
