Exact optimum in 161–223 of 300 problems. Nearest-drone rule: 94.7–95.5%.

300 random problems per fleet size, brute force over every assignment and order.

To judge CBBA, I generated 300 random problems for each fleet size and found the best possible assignment by brute force: every way of giving people to drones and every flying order. CBBA scores 98.7 to 98.8 percent of that optimum on average and finds the exact optimum in about half to three quarters of the problems. The simple rule of sending each person to the nearest drone gets about 95 percent, and much worse in bad cases. The paper only guarantees 50 percent, so this is far above the guarantee.
